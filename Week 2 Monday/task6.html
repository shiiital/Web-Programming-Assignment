Pasta Carbonara

What You Need

  • Spaghetti (200g)
  • Eggs (2 large)
  • Pancetta or bacon (100g)
  • Parmesan cheese (grated)
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. While pasta cooks, fry the pancetta until crispy in a pan.
  3. Whisk eggs with grated Parmesan and a pinch of black pepper in a bowl.
  4. Do not add the egg mixture directly to very hot pasta, or it will scramble.
  5. Toss the pasta with pancetta, then stir in the egg mixture off the heat for a creamy emulsion.
