Reading Novels

Why I Love It

I enjoy reading novels because they transport me into different worlds and allow me to experience countless adventures without leaving my chair. Each story helps me relax and sparks my imagination, making reading both entertaining and enriching.

How to Start

To begin, choose a genre that excites you, whether it’s fantasy, mystery, or romance. Visit a library or bookstore and pick a beginner-friendly novel. Many readers start with classics or popular series, and exploring terms like bildungsroman can help you understand literary traditions while discovering your own preferences.
